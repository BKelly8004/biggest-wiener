Course I · Monday

The Waiver Wire Special

Charred Whole Dog · Corn Tortilla · Chipotle Emulsion

ProteinAll-beef hot dogs
MethodGrill, crosshatch char
VesselGF corn tortillas
Time15 minutes
Ingredients — Serves 2
  • 4 all-beef hot dogs, left whole
  • 6 GF corn tortillas
  • ½ cup mayonnaise + 1–2 chipotles in adobo, blended smooth
  • 1½ cups green cabbage, very thinly shaved
  • ¼ cup pickled jalapeños
  • 2 oz cotija cheese, crumbled
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • Neutral oil, kosher salt
Preparation
01Heat a grill or cast iron skillet over high heat until smoking. Add a thin film of neutral oil if using a pan.
02Score each hot dog in a tight crosshatch pattern, cutting about ⅛-inch deep on all sides. This lets the fat render out and the surface curl and crisp as it cooks.
03Grill whole, turning every 60–90 seconds, until deeply charred and the scored cuts have opened up and blistered — about 6–8 minutes total.
04Warm tortillas directly over a gas flame or dry pan until lightly charred at the edges.
05Spread chipotle mayo on each tortilla. Lay one whole charred dog in each. Top with cabbage, jalapeños, cotija. Finish with an aggressive squeeze of lime and flaky salt.
Course II · Tuesday

Pigs in a Playoff Blanket

Upside-Down Puff Pastry Dog · Gruyère · Everything Crust

ProteinAll-beef hot dogs
MethodOven bake, inversion
VesselGF puff pastry
Time10 min + 20 min oven
Ingredients — Serves 2
  • 4 all-beef hot dogs
  • 1 sheet GF puff pastry, thawed — verify label; Pepperidge Farm is NOT GF
  • 4 slices Gruyère or Swiss, cut to fit
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp everything bagel seasoning
  • Whole grain mustard, for serving
  • Parchment paper
Preparation
01Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment and lightly grease it.
02Lay cheese slices directly on the parchment. Place one hot dog centered on top of each.
03Cut puff pastry into strips wide enough to wrap each dog with slight overlap. Drape over, tucking edges beneath. Brush generously with egg wash. Press everything bagel seasoning firmly into the surface.
04Bake 18–22 minutes until deeply golden and the cheese has crisped and caramelized at the visible edges. The color should make you slightly nervous before you pull it.
05Rest exactly 3 minutes. Slide a thin spatula fully underneath and flip cheese-side up onto a board. Serve immediately with whole grain mustard.
Course III · Wednesday

Tartare of Last Resort

All-Beef Hot Dog · Capers · Dijon · Shallot · Raw Egg Yolk

ProteinAll-beef hot dogs
MethodNo-cook · knife work
VesselRing mold, plated
Time15 minutes
Ingredients — Serves 2
  • 4 all-beef hot dogs, very finely diced — knife work only, no food processor
  • 2 tbsp shallot, minced
  • 1 tbsp capers, roughly chopped
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce ⚠️ GF (Lea & Perrins is GF)
  • ½ tsp hot sauce
  • 2 raw egg yolks
  • Cornichons and GF crackers or cucumber rounds, to serve
  • Flaky salt, black pepper, chives to finish
Preparation
01Dice hot dogs as finely as possible — small, uniform pieces. This is not the time for rustic.
02In a bowl, combine diced hot dog, shallot, capers, dijon, Worcestershire, and hot sauce. Mix gently until just combined. Season with salt and pepper. Taste. Adjust.
03Press mixture firmly into a ring mold on each plate. Smooth the top. Carefully unmold.
04Nestle one raw egg yolk into a small indent pressed into the top of each tartare.
05Finish with chives and flaky salt. Arrange cornichons and crackers or cucumber alongside. Serve immediately.
Course IV · Thursday

Fourth and Wellington

Hot Dog Wellington · Mushroom Duxelles · Prosciutto · GF Puff Pastry

ProteinAll-beef hot dogs
MethodDuxelles wrap, oven roast
VesselGF puff pastry
Time45 min prep · 25 min oven
Ingredients — Serves 2
  • 4 all-beef hot dogs, patted very dry
  • 8 oz cremini or baby bella mushrooms, very finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 4 slices prosciutto, laid flat
  • 1 sheet GF puff pastry, thawed ⚠️ verify GF label
  • 1 egg, beaten (egg wash)
  • Dijon mustard, for brushing
  • Flaky salt, black pepper
  • Whole grain mustard, to serve
Preparation
01Make the duxelles: melt butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add mushrooms and cook, stirring often, until all moisture has evaporated and the mixture is dark, dry, and deeply concentrated — about 12–15 minutes. Add garlic and thyme for the final 2 minutes. Season and cool completely.
02Brush each hot dog lightly with dijon. Lay a sheet of plastic wrap flat. Arrange 2 prosciutto slices overlapping in a rectangle. Spread a thin, even layer of cooled duxelles over the prosciutto. Place a hot dog at the edge and roll tightly using the plastic wrap to help. Twist the ends to seal. Refrigerate 15 minutes minimum.
03Preheat oven to 425°F. Cut puff pastry into rectangles large enough to wrap each prosciutto-wrapped dog. Remove plastic wrap, place the roll at the pastry edge, roll tightly, and seal the seam and ends with egg wash. Brush the entire surface with egg wash.
04Score the pastry top lightly with a knife in a crosshatch or diagonal pattern. Sprinkle with flaky salt. Place seam-side down on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
05Bake 20–25 minutes until the pastry is deeply golden and shatteringly crisp. Rest 5 minutes before slicing. Serve with whole grain mustard.
