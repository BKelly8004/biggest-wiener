The Morning
After

A review, filed late.

Service was aggressive. Eight courses, one protein, no water breaks, and a host who kept insisting each one would be the last time — it never was, same as every year. The pacing was relentless in a way that stopped being funny around course five and started being a personality trait of the restaurant.

I finished dead last with 1,327.98 points for the entire season, which is a genuinely embarrassing amount of offense to generate across fourteen weeks, and I want it on record that I've made peace with that in the same way you make peace with a hangover — not because you feel better, but because complaining stopped helping around hour six.

Would I go back. Obviously. I finished 6-8, which means statistically I will be eating here again, possibly as soon as next season, possibly hosting instead of ordering, and either way somebody's getting a hot dog wrapped in something it never asked to be wrapped in.

Three stars. The food was good. The circumstances were not.
